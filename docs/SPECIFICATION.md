# Portfolio Website — Specification

## What it is
A one-page personal website for Raheel Mughal, a Software Engineer. It lets recruiters and clients see who he is, what he has built, and how to contact him, in under a minute.

## What a visitor sees
1. **Profile card** (stays on screen on large screens): photo, name, short pitch, and buttons for GitHub, LinkedIn, email and the resume PDF.
2. **Introduction**: the title "Software Engineer", a short summary, and three facts: years of experience, number of production projects, and number of companies.
3. **Recent projects**: STREXYL, FitWorks, Cardiothene, Power Genix and SYSPOS, each with a picture and a one-line description. Clicking one opens its live website.
4. **STREXYL case study**: the problem STREXYL solves, the six most interesting things built for it, and the technology used.
5. **Experience**: the three companies he has worked for, with role and dates.
6. **Tools**: the main technologies he uses, with their logos.
7. **How I build with AI**: four short cards on how Raheel uses Claude Code, subagents, MCP servers and project memory.
8. **Contact**: a large email button plus GitHub, LinkedIn and resume links.

## Look and feel
Dark background, bold two-tone headings, orange accents, and gentle fade-in animations as you scroll. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.

## Hosting
Free hosting on GitHub Pages. Updating the site means changing the files and pushing them to GitHub.
